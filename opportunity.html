HOW IT WORKS

Five steps from call to decision.

1
Call for entries

A sponsor opens a contest and defines the brief.

2
Traders set the odds

Anyone backs the entries they believe will be selected.

3
Price becomes triage

Live odds rank every submission for reviewers.

4
Sponsor decides

The final selection stays with the sponsor, always.

5
Traders earn P&L

Correct calls settle against the sponsor's decision.
