MARKET PRIMITIVES

Every kind of question, one market engine.

Four primitives cover every shape of belief. Pick one, write the question, and the protocol builds the market.

Categorical

A single yes-or-no question with two outcomes: it either happens or it doesn't.

Example: Will Ethereum ship the Glamsterdam upgrade before July 2027?

Multi-Categorical

One question with many possible winners — the market picks which outcome lands.

Example: Which L2 leads by TVL in Q2 2027 — Arbitrum, Base, Optimism, or Other?

Scalar

A numeric range market: trade the probability that a value lands between two bounds.

Example: Where will the Fed funds rate sit at the end of Q1 2027?

Multi-Scalar

Several numeric ranges at once — forecast a whole curve, not just a point.

Example: Bitcoin's closing price on the last day of each quarter of 2026?
